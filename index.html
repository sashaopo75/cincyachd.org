About

What is ACHD?

Adult congenital heart disease, or ACHD, refers to heart conditions present from birth that continue to need specialized attention in adulthood. Many people with congenital heart disease had surgery or procedures as children, but ongoing follow-up remains important throughout life.

This site is being built as a Cincinnati-focused resource for education, community, events, publications, and personal stories.

Our community

Serious heart care. Real people. Local connection.

We are creating a welcoming place to share updates, highlight the people behind the work, and make it easier for adults with congenital heart disease in the Cincinnati region to find reliable information and support.

35th Annual International Symposium on Adult Congenital Heart Disease

June 3–6, 2026 • Hyatt Regency Cincinnati • Cincinnati, Ohio

This international ACHD symposium returns to Cincinnati with didactic and collaborative sessions for trainees, physicians, advanced practice providers, nurses, physician assistants, and other professionals caring for adults with congenital heart disease.

Key topics: heart failure, mechanical support and transplant; reproductive health and pregnancy; international perspectives; electrophysiology; multicenter research; psychology and neurodevelopment; exercise testing and rehabilitation; cardiac surgery; and transcatheter therapeutics.
